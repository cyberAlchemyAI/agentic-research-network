# Bounded work units and evidence contracts

These are candidate tasks. A task is complete only with its named artifact and evidence; a checked document is not a completed study.

| Work unit | Depends on | Output | Acceptance / handoff |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| EA-W01 accept scope | P0 proposal | Role profile, target outcome, owner and jurisdiction decisions. | D01 recorded, definition and claim ceiling updated. |
| EA-W02 complete source review | W01 | Search protocol, evidence ledger and source appraisal. | Includes contrary studies, role transfer limits and dated legal-source check. |
| EA-W03 first-session case science | Mixed-pilot scope | Case grammar, one reviewed releasable cross-domain case plus candidate variants, expert review and rubric; larger role-specific bank grows for E02/E04. | Expert panel agrees observable creative behaviors; no exposed examples scored. Bank size and role transfer are later research questions. |
| EA-W04 other session cases | Mixed-pilot scope | Technical/product and cooperation cases understandable to mixed developers, anchored pilot rubrics, and planned independent criteria/E05B-C protocols. | SME and accessibility review; work-sample and observed-cooperation comparators specified for later named-role study. |
| EA-W05 pilot protocol | W03-W04, D02 | Registered mixed-developer formative E03/E08 questions, collection/missingness plan and later E04/E05A-C/E07 modules kept separate. | Research/privacy/access review, inclusion, precision targets and stop rules before recruitment. |
| EA-W06 three-session MVP | W03-W05, D02 | L0 synthetic proof followed by L1 private three-session developer challenge and provisional feedback. | Case release for all three, purpose choices, correction, deletion, accommodation and replay journeys demonstrated before and during the permissioned pilot. |
| EA-W07 reproducible scoring | W03-W06 | Pinned ASR/AI scorer, evaluator corpus and replay receipt. | Blind human comparison and failure-case reporting; no auto rejection. |
| EA-W08 named-role measurement study | W01, W05-W07 | E01 job map and E03-E04, E05A-C and E07 run reports, separately by session. | Reliability, construct transfer, added value and subgroup uncertainty reviewed independently for all three in a defined role context. |
| EA-W09 outcome partnership | W01, D02 | Employer agreements, outcome rubric and missing-label design. | All-candidate outcome strategy or explicit inferential limit. |
| EA-W10 predictive study | W08-W09 | Frozen E06 protocol and prospective result. | Predeclared target, margins, equal-time and long-pipeline comparators, holdouts. |
| EA-W11 product decision | W10 and E08 | Scoped product posture, claim text and revised roadmap. | Owner records distinct decisions for challenge, employer aid, hiring use and publication. |

Task ordering may change if a negative result appears. Every work unit records source identities, owner, current status, evidence links, unresolved risks and next handoff. None is marked done here merely because a design exists.
