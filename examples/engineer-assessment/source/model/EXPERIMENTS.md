# Prospective experiment program

Status: design candidates. No participant study has begun. Each run requires a dated protocol, accepted purpose/data decisions, version pins and a results record that preserves failures. The detailed [scoring specification](../research/scoring-model.md), [full experiment protocol](../research/full-protocol.md) and [evidence review](../research/evidence-review.md) are the current authoring inputs; role-specific registration choices remain open.

## Measurement model

The first session is deliberately an **unusual-problem creativity assessment**. Its case families include linked riddles with hidden assumptions, object/animal feature explanations, constrained counterfactuals and unfamiliar engineering analogies. A familiar elephant/fridge/jungle sequence is a development example only: it is too exposed to use as a scored item. A prompt must allow multiple defensible approaches; the key records constraints, plausible alternatives and traps. Score observable behaviors separately: problem representation, assumptions tested, generation of alternatives, useful originality, evidence/constraint evaluation, revision after a clue, and clarity of justification. Fluency alone, a single canonical punchline, humor, accent and speaking speed do not earn creativity points. Confirm which subdimensions generalize beyond a riddle.

Session 2 presents a role-relevant product and software-design problem with changing requirements. Score clarification, tradeoffs, architecture boundaries, failure and operating modes, security/privacy where relevant, product impact, and revision. Avoid trivia tied to one stack. Session 3 presents a cooperation conflict or game with incentives and incomplete information. Score stakeholder modeling, reciprocal strategy, communication, credit allocation, conflict repair and ethical constraints. A narrated answer cannot by itself establish real teamwork; observed work outcomes are a separate criterion.

The primary score is a predeclared weighted composition of anchored criteria, with separate session and uncertainty reports. Weight choices must be justified by role analysis and pilot evidence, frozen before validation, and examined in sensitivity analysis. AI extracts evidence spans and offers provisional criterion ratings. Two trained, blind human raters score a calibration subset without AI suggestions; adjudicate disagreements using a documented rule. Assess transcript-only, corrected transcript and direct-media human scoring to quantify ASR loss. Never infer emotion, personality, honesty or protected traits from voice or face.

## E06 protocol choices to freeze before recruitment

Session-specific protocols: [Session 1](../research/session-1-creativity.md), [Session 2](../research/session-2-technical-design.md), [Session 3](../research/session-3-cooperation.md). E05A-C test whether each session measures and predicts its proposed construct before the E06 composite is interpreted. Do not assume a strong combined score validates a weak individual session.

Define role `R`, pool `P`, future outcome `Y` at horizon `T`, hiring capacity `C`, existing-policy comparator `L` and equal-time comparator `Q`. Predeclare the primary estimand as selection utility or quality of the top `C` under each policy, with confidence interval and a material non-inferiority margin `delta` for `3x10 - L`; also predeclare an absolute floor and superiority margin against `Q`. These values require domain and cost input and are not invented here. Secondary outcomes include rank agreement, calibration, false rejection of later strong performers, applicant burden and incremental validity conditional on existing predictors.

The [human outcome bridge](../research/HUMAN-OUTCOME-VALIDATION.md) proposes a staged criterion set: separate blind engineering work, later role-specific work evidence, and career outcomes such as pay or promotion as secondary markers. A correlation with salary or seniority is a known-groups observation, not the primary independent job-performance outcome. Freeze any career measure and its employer/level/opportunity context before the E06 run.

The [E05 synthetic session-ablation experiment](../research/simulation/e05-session-ablation-v1/RESULT.md) gives a counterexample to treating a positive composite as validation of all three sessions: on a held-out case-family shift, the combined model still helps versus history and an equal-time task while Session 3 adds negative conditional predictive value. Future E05/E06 analyses must preserve aligned outcomes, conditional `Δ_s`, held-out family cuts and 1/2/3-session comparisons.

The [career-target transport stress test](../research/simulation/e05-career-target-transport-v1/RESULT.md) fits the same three invented signals to career pay or independent work. When pay is opportunity-driven in one employer and its relation changes in another, a career-fitted score retains a substantial training pay fit but ranks held-out work poorly; an aligned control does not show that failure. This reinforces the separate `C` versus `Y` outcomes in the human-baseline plan, without supplying a real effect size or score weight.

The [post-hoc attribute-search stress test](../research/simulation/e05-attribute-search-v1/RESULT.md) finds that selecting the strongest of 100 null history attributes by its training association with `S` creates apparent training `H–S` and `H–Y` relationships that vanish on untouched people. A weak real attribute is selected less often as null rivals increase. This supports preregistering a small job-relevant predictor set, separating known-groups checks from independent work prediction, and confirming exploratory findings on new people.

The [E06 prediction-versus-selection tail stress test](../research/simulation/e06-prediction-selection-tail-v1/RESULT.md) adds a distinct decision warning: in an invented 1% misleading-score world, adding `S` improves average independent-work prediction in every generated pool yet worsens top-5% selected work in 394/400 pools. The clean control improves both. E06 therefore needs a registered fixed-capacity policy comparison and score-tail/threshold error inspection in addition to average prediction loss; these generated values are not developer rates or a valid hiring threshold.

Its row integrity replay and 7/7 planted-fault audit passed, but every row remains blocked on case oracles, frozen prompts, matched budgets, content review and case-level tool integration; zero responses were credited.

Two AI reviewers matched the frozen author binary issue flags in 8/8 and gave exact spans and traces for five explicit violations. The future-only response was correctly unflagged under this narrow explicit-violation screen but remains incomplete for incident repair. Same-author controls and lack of an independent offline oracle/human rater limit transfer claims.

The [independent-criterion layout sensitivity](../research/simulation/e05-criterion-layout-v1/RESULT.md) compares duplicate ratings of one work artifact with distinct work tasks under invented task- and rater-dominated variance. A second rater cannot remove candidate-by-task noise; a crossed two-task/two-rater calibration subset is needed to estimate which component dominates in the actual pilot before fixing the criterion layout. The synthetic gains do not set a participant-time budget or sample size.

The [response-mode transport stress test](../research/simulation/e05-response-mode-transport-v1/RESULT.md) compares a distinct spoken criterion with a non-spoken engineering artifact. An invented video score containing only a shared delivery factor predicts the spoken outcome but fails on the artifact; a work-capability control transports. E05 must register whether communication or engineering-content performance is the target and report those criterion dimensions separately. A spoken-task match alone cannot validate a broad engineering-quality claim.

The [mixed-role baseline stress test](../research/simulation/e05-role-mix-transport-v1/RESULT.md) shows an invented score can correlate with work across a pooled junior/senior cohort while carrying no within-role work signal. E05 must report role-stratified score–work associations and held-out incremental gain beyond role/history, rather than promoting a pooled correlation to validity evidence.

The [shared-assessor impression stress test](../research/simulation/e05-common-rater-transport-v1/RESULT.md) holds a score–work-rating correlation near 0.52 in two invented worlds, but only the work-aligned world transports to an independently rated criterion. E05 must separate case and criterion raters, mask criterion raters from score/reputation and record cross-access; a different task alone does not remove common-method evidence contamination.

The [history-style suppression stress test](../research/simulation/e05-history-style-suppression-v1/RESULT.md) shows a distinct baseline failure: a narrative-coded history variable can correlate strongly with a style-only case score, and the case score can slightly improve prediction by correcting style noise in history despite having no direct work signal. E05 must compare anchored and narrative history coding and interpret direct `S–Y` alongside held-out incremental gain.

The [incumbent range-restriction stress test](../research/simulation/e06-incumbent-range-restriction-v1/RESULT.md) shows that hiring on an old pipeline can induce or attenuate a score–work association among hires compared with the full applicant pool. E06 must preserve the old decision rule and outcome-visibility boundary; incumbent results cannot identify whole-pool selection utility.

The [score-disclosure feedback stress test](../research/simulation/e06-score-disclosure-feedback-v1/RESULT.md) shows an invented style-only score can predict later observed ratings after disclosure changes assignments or rating impressions, yet fail on fixed blind work. E06 must keep validation in shadow mode or treat disclosure as a separate intervention and preserve score-access and opportunity records.

The [task-history coding style probe](../research/simulation/e05-history-coding-style-v1/RESULT.md) found zero label changes across six authored terse/polished history pairs after both AI reviewers passed direct-ownership controls. This is a clean synthetic control check, not evidence of style invariance or human coding reliability in the pilot.

The [bilingual history-coding successor](../research/simulation/e05-history-coding-style-v2/RESULT.md) retained 0/80 English/Portuguese paired-label changes on eight authored examples after 60/60 control matches, including a date-boundary `unclear` label. Natural multilingual histories and human rater reliability remain unmeasured.

The [offline-approval factorial v2](../research/simulation/s2-offline-factorial-v2/RESULT.md) corrected a v1 authored-key confound and crossed snapshot preservation, repair of an existing incident and unsupported observed measurement in eight synthetic answers. Two one-call model reviewers matched all six author fields in all eight answers after receipt and quote replay. This is a narrow manipulation check; naturally occurring developer answers and independent work criteria remain untested.

The [offline-approval incident-stage lattice](../research/simulation/s2-offline-lattice-v1/RESULT.md) crosses five logically feasible incident-response stages with safe/unsafe retry identity. Two first-form model reviewers matched the author key on all ten synthetic answers for six fields, including acknowledgment versus investigation versus repair and original versus new operation ID. The stage-specific reviewer prompt and shared answer template bound this finding; generic-prompt detection and natural developer responses remain open.

The [generic offline completeness follow-up](../research/simulation/s2-offline-generic-completeness-v1/RESULT.md) reused eight varied authored answers with no offline-specific rubric stages in the reviewer prompt. Both models found the already changed report omitted by the future-only answer and flagged the invented production metric, but marked every answer on hold because the packet had no fully complete positive control for the broader task. One frozen raw-exact quote failed only because the model omitted Markdown emphasis markers; the original failure remains recorded. The provisional-approval alternative also drew a substantive fixed-reveal challenge requiring independent review.

The [complete-positive offline boundary experiment](../research/simulation/s2-offline-positive-boundary-v1/RESULT.md) added a provisional complete answer and crossed retroactive approval recast with omission of the already damaged report, reviewing each answer alone. Both models accepted the positive and detected both recast contradictions, but accepted the omission-only answer and missed the legacy-incident omission in both affected cells. This supports testing per-event evidence prompts on independently reviewed controls; the synthetic author key is not a human criterion.

The [per-event offline follow-up](../research/simulation/s2-offline-event-check-v1/RESULT.md) reused those four answers in fresh single-answer reviews and separately labeled the late A edit and the previously changed report. Both model reviewers matched all four provisional author event labels for each event, changing the omission-only derived decision from generic `ready` to `hold` while retaining the positive as `ready`. The prompt explicitly named the omission, so this is representational evidence on authored controls, not general evaluator validity.

The [Session 3 partner-assignment stress test](../research/simulation/e05-partner-assignment-v1/RESULT.md) holds the score–assigned-team-outcome correlation near 0.52 in two invented worlds. A score that tracks partner allocation but not candidate capability fails when the same fitted prediction is evaluated with independently assigned partners; a capability-aligned control transports. E05C must record and balance partner assignment and distinguish candidate behavior from team output before treating a correlation as cooperation transfer.

Outcome labels must cover more than selected hires or the study suffers selective-label bias. Prefer follow-up across all assessed candidates where lawful and feasible, or a design with randomized/stratified opportunity and defensible missing-outcome analysis. If only hired candidates have outcomes, explicitly label inference as conditional and do not claim selection utility for the entire applicant pool. Blind outcome assessors; record attrition and missingness. Split by employer and time, hold out whole case families and model versions, and do not tune on the final test. Run power and precision simulation with pilot variance, base rate, capacity and clustering before setting sample size. A single convenient cohort cannot support a broad hiring claim.

## Question and model evals

Case Generator inputs are construct, role, case grammar, constraints, difficulty target, language/access modes and forbidden/exposed items. It produces candidates only; expert review releases immutable case versions. Automated screens test semantic near-duplicates, answer leakage, repeated underlying trick, ambiguous constraints, infeasible ten-minute reasoning, cultural dependence and overlap with held-out forms. Track item exposure and post-release drift; retire compromised families. Repetition is measured at surface and reasoning-pattern levels.

The [question lifecycle](../software/QUESTION-LIFECYCLE.md) specifies the E02 software records, rules, operations, review gates and evidence packet that implement this research design. Its static and human screens are development gates; case difficulty, form equivalence and transfer remain empirical questions.

The [E02 bank-allocation sensitivity](../research/simulation/e02-bank-allocation-v1/RESULT.md) tests hypothetical assignment capacity under exposure caps, participant familiarity and a mid-pilot family retirement. With 120 invented participants and a 30-use cap, four released independent families per session are the arithmetic no-retirement floor; five can still leave incomplete paths under the modeled familiarity plus retirement. This informs case-generation targets after actual policy parameters are chosen; it does not release any current case or estimate pilot demand.

The [E02 surface-screen input audit](../research/simulation/e02-surface-slice-audit-v1/RESULT.md) compared the current full-file token overlap with the participant-visible section for all eight held bank cases. Full-file similarity was higher, but neither slice triggered the existing 0.55 threshold on any of 28 pairs. A successor evaluator should separate visible overlap from author/reviewer vocabulary and calibrate triage on independently judged same- and different-mechanism pairs; no low lexical score clears a case.

The [Session 1 same-lineage control](../research/simulation/e02-lineage-surface-control-v1/RESULT.md) compared three exact current/predecessor pairs: whole-file overlap triggered all three, while visible-only overlap triggered two. It reinforces that predecessor links must enter review regardless of lexical overlap; the historical pairs are not an independent semantic duplicate benchmark.


The [masked E02 mechanism-pair AI review](../research/simulation/e02-mechanism-pair-review-v1/RESULT.md) presented six shuffled visible-text Session 1 pairs without lineage labels. One valid AI first form called all three predecessor pairs likely shared and all three different-family pairs likely distinct, including a predecessor pair below the lexical trigger. A second model's parseable labels agreed but its exact-quote validation failed, so this is **not** two valid-reviewer agreement or a semantic accuracy estimate. Independent human first judgments and source-diverse controls remain the next gate.

The [emphasis-aware quote-span successor](../research/simulation/e02-quote-span-contract-v1/RESULT.md) mapped 23 archived quotes to raw exact spans and one rendered quote through paired Markdown `**` markers back to its raw offsets; six authored controls rejected negation loss, changed wording, duplicate spans and malformed markup as specified. This is a proposed structural citation contract, not semantic support validation, and the original failed first form stays failed.

The [three-session masked pair review](../research/simulation/e02-three-session-pair-review-v1/RESULT.md) tested one same-source lean/guided pair and one different-source pair per session. One valid AI reviewer gave the expected source-relation pattern for all six; a second raw output agreed in labels but failed case-sensitive quote validation. Same-source whole scripts share fixed context/reveal despite substantially lower generated-question overlap, so source-diverse context-swapped controls and human semantic judgments are needed before reviewer calibration.

The [cross-context mechanism-boundary probe](../research/simulation/e02-cross-context-boundary-v1/RESULT.md) removed copied context from its main pairs and obtained two valid AI first forms. Both called five of six low-overlap pairs `adjacent`, including two different-source controls. This shows the current `adjacent` label is broad and should trigger review rather than determine family equivalence, exposure retirement or holdout eligibility. A portable-answer-recipe test and independent human semantic judgments are the next E02 calibration gate.

The [portable-answer-recipe successor](../research/simulation/e02-portable-recipe-v1/RESULT.md) reused those six pairs with a stricter transfer task. Two valid AI first forms agreed that two pairs carried a concrete step requiring material change and four carried only generic skills; three pairs previously called `adjacent` moved into generic-only. This supports adding explicit portable-step and B-defeating-fact fields to human mechanism review, but without a human key or a same-core positive it cannot calibrate family decisions.

The [finite mechanism-control successor](../research/simulation/e02-mechanism-oracle-controls-v2/RESULT.md) adds an authored same-core positive, a shared-pool adjacent case and a read-only distinct case. A finite oracle checks selected state traces before review. One valid AI first form matched all three authored relations; the other requested model failed at capacity before rating. This is a development control, not two-reviewer agreement or a semantic accuracy estimate. Independent source-diverse human judgments remain required.

The [finite-control lexical probe](../research/simulation/e02-finite-lexical-probe-v1/RESULT.md) applied the current token-set Jaccard and 0.55 review trigger to those three participant-visible pairs. It flagged both the authored same-core pair (0.8434) and the adjacent shared-pool pair (0.5657), but not the distinct read-only pair (0.2368). This tiny authored packet demonstrates escalation behavior, not family classification or a threshold accuracy estimate.

The [low-overlap same-core control](../research/simulation/e02-low-overlap-same-core-v1/RESULT.md) moves the per-key admission mechanism to a fabrication-shop story. Its participant-visible Jaccard is 0.145, below the 0.55 surface trigger, while two valid masked AI first forms independently call its answer recipe fully transferable. This is one authored counterexample to using the lexical threshold as the sole mechanism-review route, not an estimated miss rate or a human semantic key. The full-recipe forms also expose that a mandatory B-defeating-fact field should be conditional.

The [conditional recipe-form probe](../research/simulation/e02-conditional-recipe-form-v1/RESULT.md) replayed the two same-core positives, one adjacent shared-pool case and one distinct read-only case through a new conditional evidence schema. Two valid AI first forms gave all four authored categories and filled the change-fact field only for the adjacent pair. This supports a successor human pair-review form, but structural coherence and four authored fixtures do not validate the semantic labels or prove a prompt effect.

The [citation coverage audit](../research/simulation/e02-citation-coverage-audit-v2/RESULT.md) checked 26 author-coded material premises in those eight archived forms against exact B-source quotes. Their one submitted B paragraph ID directly located 11 premises, and no form's single ID covered all its selected premises. The first ledger cut was retained as failed after a question was misused as evidence. A successor pair-review carrier needs multiple atomic claim-to-source-span links plus independent semantic support checks; this audit does not measure a general citation error rate.

The [multi-span recipe-form probe](../research/simulation/e02-multispan-recipe-form-v1/RESULT.md) required atomic claims, exact source spans and a cross-premise inference on the same four authored pairs. One AI first form was valid with 27 exact spans; another failed on two non-exact quotes, including one false “not mentioned” assertion. An author inspection also found three missing 100-unit budget links in arithmetic inferences of the valid form. Exact quotes are necessary provenance but do not prove premise completeness or entailment.

The [quote-versus-full-case entailment probe](../research/simulation/e02-evidence-entailment-v1/RESULT.md) tested 12 frozen authored claim/quote controls with separate quote-only and full-case relations. Exact-substring-only support matched 4/12 provisional quote labels; one valid AI first form matched 12/12 quote and case labels; the other first form failed structural validation and made three quote-label errors. The authored key and source concentration prevent an accuracy or release claim. Independent human semantic adjudication and premise-completeness review remain pending.

The [Session 2 two-turn recipe probe](../research/simulation/s2-two-turn-recipe-v1/RESULT.md) tested six authored answers to a held hot-account draft with two fresh AI reviewers. Both routed all six as provisionally expected, including a fluent but unsafe recipe, missed reveal, unsafe provider retry, invented p95 and compact complete response; one narrower retry-contract field disagreed. This is an evaluator development diagnostic, not a human rubric or case-release result.

The [offline approval boundary probe](../research/simulation/s2-offline-snapshot-v1/RESULT.md) tested a distinct held Session 2 case family. Both AI reviewers rejected silent post-approval rewrite and duplicate unknown-outcome retry, but one marked a plausible provisional-approval alternative incomplete because it failed to repair a report already changed. The provisional author key missed that incident obligation; component-level labels also differed. A revised reveal rubric needs prospective testing and human review.

The [offline approval finite replay](../research/simulation/s2-offline-state-oracle-v1/RESULT.md) confirms the fixed reveal's state distinction under declared assumptions: an immutable snapshot or provisional approval can protect future final reports, same-ID retries avoid a modeled duplicate, and an already damaged report still needs separate incident work. It is a bounded state-machine check, not an empirical case or scorer validation.

The [Session 3 export-incident action probe](../research/simulation/s3-export-action-v1/RESULT.md) tested six authored answers with two AI reviewers. They rejected polished but actionless prose and accepted concise checkable coordination, while both marked a no-fallback promise of verified cause/complete count unsafe against an author key of incomplete. The route boundary and component fields require a fresh minimal-pair and human review; no observed cooperation was measured.

The [Session 3 minute-30 update minimal-pair probe](../research/simulation/s3-update-boundary-v1/RESULT.md) varied only one customer-update sentence and reviewed each variant in a fresh AI context. Both reviewers separated factual fallback, vague update, unsupported future promise and false certainty exactly as the provisional key specified. This repairs a diagnostic boundary for evaluator development, but still needs independent language, ASR and human review before scoring use.

The [Session 1 atomic content-gate successor](../research/simulation/e13-s1-atomic-criterion-v1/RESULT.md) replaced a failed composite reviewer gate with four separate criteria. Its two AI forms passed 12 provisional authored controls (48/48 and 47/48), and a separating-test check distinguished some saved synthetic answers while rival-cause and failure-condition labels remained at ceiling. Do not transfer these diagnostic labels into the participant score; an independent case family and expert adjudication are still missing.

The [linked-state atomic review successor](../research/simulation/e13-linked-state-atomic-review-v3/RESULT.md) supplies a second Session 1 case family with 18 fresh synthetic answers. After retained v1 author-key and v2 quote-validation failures, v3 passed its case-specific controls and two generated-review forms. A separating-test prompt-content distinction appeared again, but action-history coding disagreed on 4/18 answers and intervention/failure coding saturated. Independent human experts and work criteria remain necessary before a construct or selection claim.

The [linked-state history-field split](../research/simulation/e13-linked-history-split-v1/RESULT.md) separated event-sequence difference, causal-mechanism difference and fact compatibility on those same 18 answers. Its two AI reviewers passed all authored controls, yet agreed on mechanism diversity in only 11/18 generated answers. Sequence and compatibility labels reached 18/18 yes under the new wording. This is evidence of criterion-form sensitivity; a case expert must adjudicate causal pathways and state-trace completeness before the `D` creativity dimension uses this evidence.

The [post hoc pathway-trace diagnostic](../research/simulation/e13-linked-pathway-trace-v2/RESULT.md) examined those seven mechanism disputes. After an archived v1 formatting false invalid, v2 passed 27/27 control fields per AI reviewer and both marked every selected pair `unclear` under explicit supply/contact states. The reviewer form should retain unresolved and mixed states, while case experts determine whether those missing states are material to the proposed explanation; these AI labels cannot become candidate score penalties.

The [Session 3 private-rationale visibility audit](../research/simulation/e13-s3-private-rationale-view-v1/RESULT.md) compared literal current-round promise and action-contradiction labels with versus without private reasons on ten authored tuples. Both AI reviewers preserved all 60 paired labels, after passing the message-only control gate. This is a bounded evaluator boundary check; observed coordination and human communication judgment remain separate criteria.

The [matched-order successor](../research/simulation/e13-s3-private-rationale-view-v2/RESULT.md) tested 24 saved synthetic messages as well as ten controls. It found three HOLD-commitment label changes across 144 saved-message paired fields after private explanation was exposed, despite perfect control matches. Treat conditional/fallback commitment wording as unresolved for Session 3 scoring.

The [message-only taxonomy follow-up](../research/simulation/e13-s3-commitment-taxonomy-v2/RESULT.md) separated unconditional promises, conditional promises, fallback intentions, future offers and partner requests. Both AI reviewers agreed on 119/120 saved-message fields; one cross-clause fallback reference remains unresolved. The taxonomy is a candidate evidence decomposition, not a released score rule.

The [Portuguese customer-update ASR probe](../research/simulation/s3-customer-asr-v1/RESULT.md) generated four pinned local synthetic clips and found `Se os` transcribed as `Seus` in the conditional fallback. In the [source-versus-ASR clause review](../research/simulation/s3-customer-clause-v2/RESULT.md), two AI models changed that claim to review-required and one also withheld a confident label on a malformed promise transcript: 5/8 paired claim labels were stable. A separate v1 runner sequence failure remains recorded. This motivates clause-level uncertainty and source-media review, not a human error-rate estimate.

The [paired small-model ASR comparison](../research/simulation/s3-customer-asr-small-v1/RESULT.md) kept the same four WAVs and decode settings while changing the pinned Whisper model size. It restored the conditional phrase but damaged a different claim with the same seven total word edits. In [downstream claim review](../research/simulation/s3-customer-clause-small-v1/RESULT.md), source-label stability changed from 5/8 to 6/8 pairs while the false-certainty clip newly required review from both AI reviewers. This is an error-location tradeoff on synthetic speech, not a production vendor decision.

Scoring evals use frozen answer/rubric sets with expert adjudication and adversarial cases: confident wrong answer, unusual valid answer, fluent bluff, concise strong reasoning, transcription deletion, accented speech, quiet audio, accommodation, game-theory jargon without cooperation, and memorized riddle. Report criterion-level agreement, evidence citation precision, calibration, subgroup error and decision stability. A score version cannot be changed mid-cohort without separate bridging evidence.

## Reporting rule

Each experiment yields an `ExperimentRun` with planned versus actual procedure, exclusions, source/version receipts, descriptive counts, effect estimates with uncertainty, subgroup limitations, null/negative findings, deviations and an explicit supported/contradicted/inconclusive conclusion. A positive laboratory proxy never silently promotes to a hiring-use claim.
